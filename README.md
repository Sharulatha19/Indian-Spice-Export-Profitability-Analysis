# Indian-Spice-Export-Profitability-Analysis
This project focuses on analyzing a simulated Indian spice export dataset using Python with a final dashboard in Power BI. The analysis explores sales performance, revenue, costs, profit and loss, spice types, Indian origin states, customer regions and countries, sales channels, and order priorities in both US dollars and Indian rupees. It identifies which spices and states are most profitable, which ones make losses or earn thin margins, and how exchange rates and seasonality affect results. 
✨Key objectives
Measure total revenue, cost and profit in USD and INR.
Find the most and least profitable states and spices.
Identify loss-making orders and low-margin products.
Compare online and offline sales channels.
Analyze demand by customer region and country.
Study yearly and monthly trends and seasonality.
Examine the effect of the exchange rate on rupee profit.
Check whether order priority affects shipping time.
